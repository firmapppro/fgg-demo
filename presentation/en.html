Our approach

Access to a thousand aircraft
does not make choosing easier.

The business aviation market is vast. A single route can have dozens of aircraft of different class, year, condition, cabin configuration and cost. Our job is not to shift that choice onto the client.

Aircraft selection

We do not work from a catalogue.
We work with specific aircraft.

Thousands of aircraft are available on the market. The client does not need to choose from thousands of options. Our team builds its own working pool of aircraft of different classes. Today it includes more than 20 specific aircraft that we have reviewed, selected and use to fulfil our clients’ requests.

What matters is not only the type — a Citation XLS+, a Challenger 350 or a Gulfstream G550 — but the specific aircraft. For each one we look beyond the technical specification to how it actually performs in service.

  • Cabin configuration and condition
  • Year of manufacture and interior updates
  • Baggage capacity
  • Service on board
  • Operator specifics
  • Geography and operating capability
  • Experience from completed flights
  • Feedback from our clients

Scenario 01

Cessna Citation XLS+

08:30. Central Europe.
You and a partner are in one of the cities. This afternoon you need to be at a meeting in another city. There are two of you.

That is how the task sounds to the client. Then our team goes to work. We identify the nearest convenient departure airport, a convenient arrival airport, actual aircraft availability and the total travel time.

For a route like this, one of the suitable solutions can be a specific 2016 Citation XLS+ that our team knows and that is not far from the departure point. It can use regional airports, gives the comfort two passengers need, and does not carry the surplus cost of a larger aircraft.

Scenario 02

A different trip — a different solution.

Dubai → family trip · 6 passengers · children · substantial baggage · long-range routing

If the task changes, the aircraft changes. Cabin space, the ability to rest properly, baggage volume and completing the route without an extra stop come to the fore.

So a suitable solution may already be an aircraft of a different class — a Gulfstream G550, for example. The aircraft is defined by the conditions of the trip, not by the status of the model or the lowest price.

Gulfstream G550

Long-range class · spacious cabin · large baggage hold · the ability to rest in flight

Scenario 03

Bombardier Global Express

Evening in Europe. Tomorrow morning: negotiations on another continent.
Four on board. Work to be done in flight, with no stop.

A tight schedule, a long distance, and the need to arrive ready for the meeting. For a task like this we may recommend an ultra-long-range aircraft: a Global Express gives range, space to work and a nonstop flight — without a connection and without losing a day.

The difference is not aircraft class as status. The difference is the task — and which specific aircraft our team is prepared to recommend for it.
