Our approach

An aircraft is not the product.
It is a tool for your task.

The business aviation market provides access to thousands of aircraft. But a client does not need the entire market — they need one right option for a specific trip.

The choice depends on aircraft position, airports, schedule, range, cabin, passengers, baggage, operator and cost. So FGG does not shift the comparison onto the client: we build a short list of solutions and explain how each one differs.

You are not choosing an aircraft. You are choosing a solution to your task.

We know the aircraft we offer.

Through FGG’s global network, thousands of aircraft are accessible. Only a selected part enters our app — specific aircraft and operators the team knows well enough to recommend to a client.
