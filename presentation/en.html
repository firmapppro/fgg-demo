Our approach

Access to a thousand aircraft
does not make choosing easier.

The business aviation market is vast. A single route can have dozens of options — different in aircraft position, airports, preparation time, range, cabin, baggage capacity and cost.

FGG’s job is not to shift that choice onto the client. We reduce the market to a few options and explain the logic of each: what it actually gives within this specific trip.

So the client gets a few clear options — not the market.

Aircraft selection

Scenario 01

Cessna Citation XLS+

08:30 · Central Europe
Two passengers. A meeting in another city this afternoon.

The key task is to minimise total travel time and arrive as close as possible to the meeting.

For a route like this, one of the optimal solutions can be a Citation XLS+: a comfortable cabin for two passengers, sufficient range, and the ability to use convenient regional airports — without the surplus capabilities of a larger aircraft.
