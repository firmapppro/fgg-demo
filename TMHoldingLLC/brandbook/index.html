Corporate Mandate & Heritage

02

TM Holding LLC is a prestigious holding enterprise incorporated in the Qatar Financial Centre (QFC), Doha.

The company operates at the pinnacle of movable asset investment, dedicating institutional capital to the acquisition, syndication, and long-term commercial leasing of business aircraft and luxury maritime vessels.

This manual codifies the visual identity and precision standards required across all international jurisdictions, regulatory filings, OEM certifications (including Bombardier and Rolls-Royce), and client communications.

02. Logotype

Monogram Geometry & Anatomy

03

The TM Monogram is an interlocking precision mark fusing three core institutional pillars:

1. The Swept Wing (T Crossbar): Evokes aerodynamic efficiency, private aviation elevation, and long-range flight vision.

2. The Hydrodynamic Keel (M Spires): Dual upward architectural columns cutting into an angular maritime prow, symbolizing luxury seafaring and deep ocean stability.

3. The Interlocking Keystone (T & M Nexus): The vertical spine of the T anchors directly into the center of the M, representing institutional capital security.

02. Logotype

Clear Space & Minimum Scale

08
SAFE ZONE: 1.5X

To maintain institutional authority and uncompromised optical clarity, the TM monogram must always be surrounded by an exclusionary margin of at least 1.5X the height of the central T keystone.

Minimum Digital Size: 24px × 24px (favicon/mobile indicator).

Minimum Print Size: 12mm × 12mm (business cards / micro documents).
