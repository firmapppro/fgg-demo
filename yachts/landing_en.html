About this superyacht

Benetti Oasis 40M is a new word in the world of luxury charter vessels. The main feature of the model is a revolutionary open aft area with opening sides, transforming into a huge beach club right at the water's edge with its own glass panoramic pool.

The interiors are custom-made by a leading Italian design bureau, combining expensive woods, premium leather, and floor-to-ceiling panoramic glazing around the entire perimeter of the main deck. The perfect choice for sophisticated clients who value uncompromising comfort and privacy.
