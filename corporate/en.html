Position

We do not promise “any airline at the best price”.

On a group charter, the lowest opening figure is often not a good price. A quote can omit aircraft positioning, slots, ground handling, airport constraints, crew, baggage, timetable changes or the specifics of the programme. The number that looked attractive at the start can move while the flight is still being prepared.

We work differently.

First we check whether the programme can be operated the way the client needs it. Then we propose an aircraft and a cost based on the real conditions of the flight.

We propose operators we are prepared to stand behind.
