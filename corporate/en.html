Our approach

A group charter starts with the brief, not with a hunt for an aircraft.

Finding an empty aircraft and quoting a price is not enough for a group movement. Route, passenger count, departure and arrival times, airport availability and the requirements of the flight itself all have to fit.

FlightAero first shapes a suitable flight scenario, then selects the aircraft and operator to match it.

One request. One agreed scenario. One FlightAero team.
