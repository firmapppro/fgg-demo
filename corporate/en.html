How we work

The flight is built as an operating scenario.

Cost matters, but it follows the logic of the day. We compare options on timetable, headcount, cargo, ground and a closed handling loop — then lock a scenario that can actually be flown.

01

Brief

Dates, cities, headcount, cargo, hard arrival window. The client states what has to happen on the ground.

02

Transfer architecture

One aircraft or a pair. Leadership split from the main group when that is tighter on time and cleaner as a flow.

03

Slots and clearances

Airports, windows, cargo, equipment. Until this is locked, a found aircraft is still not a completed flight.

04

Departure day

The group moves as one flow: a single manifest, a dedicated handling loop, a boarding timeline everyone can follow.

05

Arrival and ground

Transfer, hotel, next fixture or next slot. The movement closes with handover on the ground, not when the wheels touch down.
